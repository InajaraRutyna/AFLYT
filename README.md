# Små scener

Short standalone scenes built on chapter 1 (background, how long you stay, the neighbourhood). Each scene is a small situation with a surprise at the end. The Danish is the way through: put sentences in order, pick the right form, listen and answer. Mistakes come back at the end of the scene and in the repeat round.

- `src/story.html` is the source; `python3 tools/build.py` writes `index.html`.
- `python3 test/play.py` plays all six scenes, the miss rounds and the repeat round on a phone-sized screen.
